OpenClaw

OpenClaw is Dave's personal, self-hosted AI assistant. It helps Dave read, organize, draft, and send his own email. It is not a public service and is used only by its owner.

Privacy policy

Privacy policy

  • OpenClaw accesses Gmail data (messages, labels, and the account email address) only to perform tasks the owner requests.
  • Gmail data is used only by Dave's self-hosted assistant. It is not shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party, and is not used for advertising.
  • Only the owner (Dave) uses this app. No other users' data is collected.
  • Outgoing email requires the owner's explicit approval before it is sent.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
